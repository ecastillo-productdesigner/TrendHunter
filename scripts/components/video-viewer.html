Trending Videos overlay

Clicking a video body in the Trending Videos carousel opens a full-screen modal on desktop and mobile. The existing live card moves into the viewer, preserving its playback position, mute, Share, Bookmark, author and Go deeper article link. A same-size placeholder keeps the feed and carousel scroll positions stable. The header includes the centered Trend Hunter wordmark and Log In; it has no Menu, hamburger, search or subscription action. The close button uses the shared 24px Drawer close SVG in a 48px target. It sits above Previous / Next in the same column beside the portrait video, aligned with the header on wide screens. On narrow and portrait screens it sits below the header so Log In stays accessible. Close and Escape return focus to the original Watch video control. The native modal traps focus and makes the underlying feed inactive.

The dark media surface is retained in both themes. A centered portrait video fills the viewer height on desktop; narrow screens fill the available width and height with a cover crop. The header and lower copy use black fades for legibility. Existing volume, share, bookmark, writer avatar and Go deeper controls stay in the media rail. Previous / Next buttons use the standard Google arrow SVG rotated upward/downward, with disabled end states. Arrow Up / Down, vertical wheel gestures and vertical swipes browse the four supplied clips. Clicking the open video pauses/resumes; playback and sound remain keyboard accessible. There is no progress bar or seek slider. Other clips pause while the viewer is open, and hiding the page pauses the current clip. Reduced-motion visitors keep paused carousel previews; explicitly opening Watch video starts playback.

Browsing slides the current video upward and the next video in from below; Previous reverses the movement. Buttons, arrow keys, vertical wheels and swipes use the same full-height 650ms transition (--motion-video-navigation) with --motion-ease-video-navigation. Both cards remain fully opaque, without fades or blur, and the header and navigation stay fixed. Rapid inputs cannot stack transitions. Closing mid-slide cancels the motion and restores both original cards to their feed slots. Reduced-motion preferences switch clips immediately.

Header gutters use --spacing-layout-lg, changing to --spacing-component-ms on phones. The shared wordmark dimensions, --icon-size-lg close artwork, --icon-size-2xl targets, --stroke-default borders, --radius-circle navigation controls, --color-black media backdrop and fixed authentication/media text colors reuse DS tokens. This is a local video overlay over the feed, not a separate article or third-party video service.
