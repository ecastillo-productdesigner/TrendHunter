Prediction results in sidebars

Outside the feed, the desktop results announcement sits immediately above the voting deck and below Trend Hunter Answers at the top of the right sidebar. Both prediction components remain visible and interactive. The compact horizontal card puts the Card headline and primary See results button on the left, with counted purple sheets on the right. It reuses the feed banner’s 16px padding and column gap, 12px copy-to-button gap, 104px illustration with 8px insets, shared surface, border and 2px radius. Its solid purple sheets retain the 4 / 5 ratio, fixed lime number, Verified label and static rotations. The purple stack aligns with the top of the heading rather than the center of the whole copy-and-button group; a 4px optical offset (8px below 600px) aligns the rotated sheets with the heading’s visible letters. The heading uses --type-card-headline (28px/28px, weight 800) on desktop and mobile. The button uses the active theme’s primary colors.

Show Prediction Results Card in prototype settings activates three fictional results in place on desktop article, collection and channel pages. A 24px gap (--spacing-layout-lg) separates the announcement from the prediction panel; while it is present, the deck’s outer frame is transparent to match the reference. See results opens the existing result popup. The unread count and sheet count decrease as results are viewed; after the last unread result, only the announcement disappears. The initialized voting deck stays available throughout, retaining calls, skips and context. Reloads, return visits and other tabs use the same unread state. The feed keeps its horizontal banner first, above Your Digest and every other feed card, and mobile keeps its existing inline voting placement and dedicated results destination. Pages without a desktop prediction sidebar open the feed preview.
