Share menu

The second feed card uses the supplied custom Share PNG, centered in its 32px action box above Bookmark in the right action rail. It opens this menu beside that icon. Share icons open one contextual menu with Copy link, Email and More. The menu sits 8px beside the trigger, preferably to its left, and stays inside the viewport above card clipping. It uses the surface background, default border, secondary Inter text, default-color icons, a 152px width, 12px outer padding, 46px rows and a 12px icon-to-label gap. Hover uses the tertiary hover color: white at 15% in dark mode.

Open the working share menu

Copy link confirms success. Email prepares a draft with the relevant title and URL. More opens native sharing where supported, with a copyable-link dialog as fallback. Outside click, scrolling, Tab and Escape close the menu. Arrow keys and Home/End move between options; Escape restores focus to Share. Story links use their matching article; each Answers response shares its own question URL.
