Share · system menu

Share opens the device’s native sharing menu directly from story cards, predictions, articles, collections and Answers. It shares the relevant title and link. Available destinations and appearance come from the device and browser; on macOS these may include AirDrop, Mail, Messages and Notes. The app does not recreate system icons or extension settings.

Share uses the same interactive states across stories, predictions, article headers and Answers. Default Share icons are outlined with the bookmark stroke weight and keep their gray or photo-card color. Hover turns the arrow white in dark mode and dark on light page surfaces, with no background. Pressing fills the arrow and turns it #BEFC4F immediately; it stays filled lime while the native share request or fallback dialog is open, including during hover or keyboard focus. Dismissal restores the outlined arrow in its default or hover color. Keyboard focus adds a 2px ring with a 4px offset: lime on dark surfaces, accessible green on light page surfaces, and lime on photo and purple prediction cards in both themes. Icon size and position stay fixed throughout.

Interactive states

Cancelling the system menu returns to the page. Browsers without native sharing open a copyable-link dialog with Copy link and Email; a successful copy shows Link Copied in lime for 5 seconds, with no success toast. The dialog uses the same 24px drawer-close-icon SVG mask as the drawer, centered in its 32px close button. The accessible label is Close share options; closing returns focus to Share.

Every story card uses the matching crisp SVG share arrow in its 30px action box, with a 20px-wide arrow aligned to Bookmark’s visible left and right edges on desktop and mobile, with 24px between the action boxes. Prediction cards place the same icon in the top-right corner. Article-header Share and Bookmark icons turn primary text color on hover and keyboard focus (white in dark mode), with no background; saved bookmarks retain their active lime fill when not hovered, and Share stays lime while its menu is open. The bookmark turns white on hover and keyboard focus. The default Share color matches the unsaved bookmark; hover and keyboard focus turn the arrow white without a background. While the share menu is open, its trigger stays #BEFC4F; closing it restores the default or hover color. It opens this menu beside that icon. Share icons open one contextual menu with Copy link, Email and More. The menu sits 8px beside the trigger, preferably to its left, and stays inside the viewport above card clipping. It uses the surface background, default border, secondary Inter text, default-color icons, a 176px width, 12px outer padding, 46px rows and a 12px icon-to-label gap. Hover changes the label and icon to white in dark mode, with no background.

Open the saved custom menu

A successful copy keeps the menu open and changes Copy link to Link Copied, with a lime label and link icon for 5 seconds (the app toast duration). It then resets to Copy link. No success toast appears; closing the menu clears the confirmation. The width reserves room for both labels so it stays unchanged during feedback. Email prepares a draft with the relevant title and URL. More opens native sharing where supported, with a copyable-link dialog as fallback. Outside click, scrolling, Tab and Escape close the menu. Arrow keys and Home/End move between options; Escape restores focus to Share. Story links use their matching article; each Answers response shares its own question URL.
