Share menu

Every story card uses the matching crisp SVG share arrow in its 30px action box, with a 20px-wide arrow aligned to Bookmark’s visible left and right edges on desktop and mobile. Prediction cards place the same icon in the top-right corner. The bookmark turns white on hover and keyboard focus. The default Share color matches the unsaved bookmark; hover and keyboard focus turn the arrow white without a background. While the share menu is open, its trigger stays #BEFC4F; closing it restores the default or hover color. It opens this menu beside that icon. Share icons open one contextual menu with Copy link, Email and More. The menu sits 8px beside the trigger, preferably to its left, and stays inside the viewport above card clipping. It uses the surface background, default border, secondary Inter text, default-color icons, a 176px width, 12px outer padding, 46px rows and a 12px icon-to-label gap. Hover changes the label and icon to white in dark mode, with no background.

Open the working share menu

A successful copy keeps the menu open and changes Copy link to Link Copied, with a lime label and link icon for 5 seconds (the app toast duration). It then resets to Copy link. No success toast appears; closing the menu clears the confirmation. The width reserves room for both labels so it stays unchanged during feedback. Email prepares a draft with the relevant title and URL. More opens native sharing where supported, with a copyable-link dialog as fallback. Outside click, scrolling, Tab and Escape close the menu. Arrow keys and Home/End move between options; Escape restores focus to Share. Story links use their matching article; each Answers response shares its own question URL.
