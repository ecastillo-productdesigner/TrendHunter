Collection interest indicators

Collections use four logo-inspired chevrons in a 20 × 14px box. Green points right for rising interest; red points left for falling interest. A 0–100 score determines the illuminated count: 0–25 lights one, greater than 25 through 50 lights two, greater than 50 through 75 lights three, and greater than 75 through 100 lights all four. Remaining chevrons use a 22% tint. Boundaries belong to the lower band, with scores clamped to 0–100. The same score and level apply across pills, story labels, collection summaries and dynamically followed collections. Collection detail headings have no chevrons: the indicator appears next to the signed percentage in the summary below the description. Accessible labels and tooltips retain direction and the sample percentage. These are illustrative prototype scores, not live analytics.

Quiet activity · prototype samples

Only Evidence-Based Beauty, Retailtainment Hubs, Sleep Medicine and Back-to-School have sample fluctuations. At most one visible collection changes every --motion-momentum-refresh (10 seconds), alternating up or down by one illuminated segment with a --motion-momentum-change (1200ms) fade. Duplicate instances share their state. Other collections stay still; direction, size and position remain fixed. Off-screen groups are skipped, background tabs pause and reduced-motion disables updates. Accessible percentages and tooltips follow the sample state without live-region announcements. These are illustrative sequences, not measured real-time analytics; production must supply measured score changes.

Story collection link

The collection link above a story title changes its label to primary text on hover and keyboard focus: white in dark mode, dark in light mode. There is no underline, pill outline or background, and the interest indicator retains its signal color. Light surfaces darken the percentage with existing DS color roles for readability. Screen-reader labels and tooltips state Interest rising or Interest falling, so direction is also available without color.
