Collection and channel heading arrows

Collections use green up arrows or red down arrows, with each collection keeping its assigned sample direction everywhere it appears. The heading arrow follows the last word with an 8px gap. Keep that word and the arrow together so the arrow cannot wrap onto a line by itself. Earlier words wrap normally.
