Collection interest indicators

Collections use a directional bar and percentage in pills, story labels and mastheads. Rising uses DS lime (#BEFC4F); falling uses DS red (#EF4444). A 48 × 6px track has 2px corners, a tinted remainder and a 72% accent segment anchored on opposite sides for the two directions. This bar signals direction rather than percentage progress. The 14px bold Barlow Condensed percentage sits 12px from the bar; the label-to-indicator gap is 8px. Pills use 16px side padding. Narrow layouts wrap the indicator as one unit. The same collection keeps its direction everywhere. Each collection has a stable, varied illustrative percentage (for example 22%, 17%, 9% or 20%), not live analytics or a measured time period.

Story collection link

The collection link above a story title changes its label to primary text on hover and keyboard focus: white in dark mode, dark in light mode. There is no underline, pill outline or background, and the interest indicator retains its signal color. Light surfaces darken the percentage with existing DS color roles for readability. Screen-reader labels and tooltips state Interest rising or Interest falling, so direction is also available without color.
