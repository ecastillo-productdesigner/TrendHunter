The signed-in profile icon opens the shared right-hand drawer. Its header places the most recently earned visible profile badge beside “Hello, [name]”, vertically centered with a 12px gap. With no earned badges, a gray First Call medal appears; clicking it expands the short requirement “Vote on your first prediction” and a Start collecting badges link to Daily Predictions. Earned medals show their own collection requirement. The header respects hidden badge choices and refreshes its name and medal whenever the drawer opens. A shared divider separates the header from Log Out, which clears personal prototype activity, ends the sign-in session and returns to the feed. Saved bookmarks, follows, interests, recommendation and article feedback, comments, badge progress, prediction calls/results/skips, digest catch-up state, update markers and onboarding drafts are reset. Other open or restored tabs also clear their old session activity and sign out, so stale votes and bookmarks cannot restore badge progress. After signing in again, Your Digest, Following and Collections show their no-follows empty states and no badges are collected. The display name, uploaded photo and theme preference are retained. Predictions restart with the introductory cover and unanswered deck. The shared close icon, Escape key and scrim dismiss the drawer.
