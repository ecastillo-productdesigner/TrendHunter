Desktop detail sections

At 960px and above, the middle column scrolls with the document. Article, Shopping and Trend Hunter Answers use content-sized panels with 1px borders, 2px corners, 24px padding and 24px between sections. Their top edges stay below the sticky menu as they scroll. Side rails remain sticky.

More Stories on articles, collections and channels has no outer frame or inset. Each editorial card fills the middle-column width like the feed, with 24px between cards. Collection and channel overviews have their own bordered panel above this section, separated by 24px. Collection lists include every assigned story, including article-primary assignments, with no four-story cap; the followed feed uses the same membership catalogue.

Mobile pages and article sheets keep their inline modules and section dividers.
