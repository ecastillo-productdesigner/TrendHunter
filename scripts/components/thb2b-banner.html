Trend Hunter for Business banner

A presentation-only business strip sits above the shared header and scrolls away before the sticky navigation. Explore Solutions is a static label with no link, click action or keyboard stop. Its live rollover uses --auth-email-bg-hover with --color-text-on-media (white), and --motion-hover (150ms). The close control dismisses the strip for this browser tab’s session, including subsequent page visits. Prototype settings → Show THB2B Banner restores it and brings it into view.

All visual values reuse DS tokens: --color-background-bg-overlay (#343841), --color-icon-on-media (#D1D5DB), --auth-prompt-secondary-bg and --toast-border for the outlined CTA; --type-collection-label for the title, --type-helper for the description, and --type-button-label for Explore Solutions. Desktop side padding and content gaps use --spacing-layout-lg (24px), vertical padding uses --spacing-component-sm (8px), and the CTA uses 8px / 16px padding. Below 960px the description is hidden, side padding becomes --spacing-component-ms (12px), and the title/CTA gap becomes --spacing-layout-md (16px). The existing close mask is 20px in a 48px target. Borders and corners use --stroke-default and --radius-x-sm. Back navigation uses --color-icon-default for the arrow and label, changing to --color-text-primary (white in dark mode) on hover or keyboard focus. Both rollovers are available under Buttons and states. No new color, typography or spacing values are introduced.
