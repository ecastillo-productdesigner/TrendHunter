Predictions opens from the existing profile tabs. Personal profiles filter calls by All, Active and Resolved. After a first recorded call, three illustrative historical results populate Resolved with muted You were in / You were out tags. Signed-in author profiles use By You and By [first name] in that position. Signed-out readers see the author’s predictions directly, without those filters. Posts, Saved and Collections remain separate panels. The controls below preview selected states; the linked profiles show the full lists.

Compact filters keep the existing 16px type. They use component/xs (4px) vertical padding, layout/md (16px) horizontal padding, layout/xl (32px) minimum height and component/sm (8px) between pills. The controls wrap on narrow screens instead of stretching across the row. Signing in from Predictions returns to that section with By You available.

Results reuse the shared headline, Read more, badge and split bar. Active calls use You’re in / You’re out. Resolved calls use muted You were in / You were out badges. Author histories show the author’s first name on the badge. Personal histories pair recorded calls with three illustrative past results once voting activity exists. With no votes, show the shared No predictions yet empty state and hide the status filters. Author histories remain fictional examples.
