Onboarding selection uses the 140px portrait. Great Picks uses --avatar-size-lg (96px), with two trendsetters per row. Names and uppercase role titles appear below each portrait. A single or final unpaired trendsetter remains centered.

These are static appearance samples. Actual topic and trendsetter controls expose their selected state with aria-pressed. Great Picks displays saved selections without selection rings or hover effects.

The app also defines --avatar-radius-soft (2px) for small rounded details, and uses --radius-circle (50%) for comment portraits. These previews reuse existing tokens; the 24px, 32px and 48px avatar sizes currently share the icon-size scale. Portraits are fictional prototype identities.
