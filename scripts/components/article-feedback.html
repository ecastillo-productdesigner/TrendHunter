Article trend feedback

Regular articles and prediction analysis pages ask “What did you think about this trend?” immediately before the members-exploring strip. Thumbs reuse the outlined Material Symbols thumb_up / thumb_down icons, 20px in separate 48px targets. Selection fills the icon with the theme’s accessible accent; the opposite choice clears and selecting the same thumb again removes it. Signed-out readers can also leave feedback. Sentiment persists first; an accessible modal then offers optional reasons and a comment of up to 1,000 characters. Positive and negative choices have matching reason sets. Submit saves the optional details and confirms with a toast. Cancel, Escape or outside click close the form while retaining the thumb rating; focus returns to that thumb. Failures are announced without claiming success. These prototype responses are private to this browser and article, with no network submission.

The prompt uses Body; the form uses Body and Helper text, surface and text color tokens, 24px dialog padding, 16px form gaps, 8px chip gaps, default borders, 4px chip corners and 10px dialog corners. Submit reuses the lime primary button and Cancel reuses the secondary button. Keyboard focus and theme colors match existing controls.
