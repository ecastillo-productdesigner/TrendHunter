All open predictions

The standalone Daily Predictions page has a secondary View all open predictions action with the catalogue count, 24px below the stack. It remains visible after the daily deck becomes results. The directory includes today’s ten predictions and four earlier, still-open examples, sorted by closing date. Each row shows its collection, closing date, published date and actual saved call, or Not yet voted. All / Not yet voted reuse the profile filter controls and retain their selection on reload and Back. Make your call and View your call open the matching article with that exact prediction first; saved votes stay locked. Earlier predictions enter the article only when selected and do not enlarge the daily stack. Personal vote history includes calls made on these earlier predictions. Dates and audience splits are illustrative prototype data as of November 3, 2026.
