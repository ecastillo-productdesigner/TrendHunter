What readers are saying

A quote-first feed module previews one randomly selected comment from each of three different stories in For You and logged-out Discover. It uses the same browser-local discussion data as article Comments, including current counts. Deleted and reported comments and replies are not selected as quotes. Repeated sample text and authors are avoided where possible. Choices remain stable during browsing and a new feed visit can sample other discussions. No timer refreshes the module.

Each preview shows the comment, reader avatar and name, a square story thumbnail, headline, comment count and Join the conversation link. Article links open the matching #comments section. Join the conversation is a text link without an arrow. There is no Explore control. The frame uses --stroke-default, --radius-x-sm and --spacing-layout-lg padding (24px), reducing to --spacing-layout-md (16px) on phones. Header, quote, headline, identity and action use the existing Section heading, Body, Section heading bold, Helper and Editorial h4 roles. Avatar and chat sizes reuse --icon-size-lg (24px) and --icon-size-sm (16px); story thumbnails are 64px square. A 24px divider gap separates previews. Counts include non-deleted replies, matching the article. The examples are fictional; this is not a live community API.
