Member achievement badges

Rounded-square badges use a lime #BEFC4F inset border and Material Symbols over a slate #343841 fill, in both themes. Celebration frames use --radius-lg (32px) inside --radius-xl (40px) popups. The full badge is 72 × 72px with a 32px icon and --radius-md corners; the current profile badge is 48 × 48px with a 24px icon, overlaid on the lower-right of the 140px avatar. Only the newest visible earned badge appears there. Clicking it opens a square detail popup with the shared --radius-xl (40px) radius, a 6px --stroke-weight-6 lime border inset by 8px --spacing-component-sm and the shared --color-scrim backdrop. Desktop details use DS Page masthead (40px/36px) for the name, 18px/28px Inter for the description, 14px/22px for both dates and the Show other badges action. On phones the name uses Card headline (28px/28px), Body criteria (16px/22px), Helper metadata and Show other badges (12px/20px), keeping a 48px action target. The symbol is 144px, or 80px on phones. The detail layout spaces the heading, large symbol, description and helper text down the square, with a 48px circular close button overlapping the top-right border at zero top/right insets, using the slate fill, a 3px --stroke-thick accent ring and the shared white 24px close icon. The button stays fully visible over the corner, with a circular focus outline. Show other badges sits near the bottom, with a 20px share icon in a 48px target to its right on your own earned badge details. The owner-only share control uses the existing device share flow and a link to the badge catalogue. Other people’s badges never show Share. Other members’ badge details offer the close icon only and say [Name] earned this badge by, followed by the completed action. On your own badges, Show other badges flips the same popup to the five-badge collection, distinguishing lime earned icons from gray icons and names for unearned badges. Only earned badges show an Earned label; unearned badges omit To collect. Unearned details also use gray for their icon, heading and inset border. The collection grid sits 56px below Member badges (--spacing-page-2xl plus --spacing-component-sm) on desktop and mobile. It uses aligned two-line name slots, a centered final pair, and consistent state baselines, with 48px between rows on desktop and 32px on phones. The overview has the same Page masthead heading as detail views and a Flip back action at the bottom; selecting an earned badge opens its details. It grows to fit the grid on smaller screens. Selecting a badge flips to its details. Unearned details say Earn this badge by followed by the requirement, without progress counts or Not yet collected text. Earned details retain their date; Descriptions wrap at word boundaries with up to 35 characters per line; narrower screens may wrap sooner. First Call says You earned this badge because you voted on your first prediction. The opening reveal matches the incoming view flip: 300ms, half of --motion-card-flip, ease-out, without intermediate stops or overshoot. Outside clicks, including transparent rounded corners, the close icon and Escape dismiss it. Reduced motion skips flipping. A new award opens a centered slate announcement card with the shared scrim, lime 6px inset frame and a component-only 40px radius preview. The 80px badge symbol sits above its Card headline name, followed by a Body explanation of why it was earned. A 32px visible circular close button with a 16px X retains a 48px target. The same medal burst radiates 24 lime, purple and mint particles for 2500ms (2.5 seconds) after the opening flip. The announcement stays until closed with the X, outside click or Escape, without a toast. A later award replaces the previous celebration; dismissing or replaying a celebration never changes earned progress. Reduced motion shows the announcement without animation or particles. Reset Badges clears personal progress without erasing saved calls or bookmarks; existing votes and saves become a baseline and do not re-award on reload. Public reader achievements are fictional fixtures in badge-catalog.js and separate from personal activity.
