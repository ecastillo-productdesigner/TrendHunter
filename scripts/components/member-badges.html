Member achievement badges

Rounded-square badges use a lime #BEFC4F inset border and Material Symbols over a dark forest #1C2410 fill, in both themes. The full badge is 72 × 72px with a 32px icon and --radius-md corners; the current profile badge is 48 × 48px with a 24px icon, overlaid on the lower-right of the 140px avatar. Only the newest visible earned badge appears there. Clicking it opens a square detail popup with --radius-full (100px) corners, a 3px --stroke-thick inset lime border and the shared --color-scrim backdrop. The name uses Card headline; the symbol is 144px (80px on phones); criteria use Body (16px/22px Inter); progress and earned dates use Helper copy (12px/20px). The detail layout spaces the heading, large symbol, description and helper text down the square, with Close and Show all side by side near the bottom. Other members’ badge details offer Close only. On your own badges, Show all flips the same popup to the five-badge collection, distinguishing lime earned icons from gray icons and names for badges to collect. The collection view uses aligned two-line name slots, a centered final pair, and consistent state baselines, with 24px between rows on desktop and 16px on phones. Selecting any badge flips to its criteria and progress or earned date. Show recent returns to the newest visible earned badge. The opening reveal matches the incoming view flip: 300ms, half of --motion-card-flip, ease-out, without intermediate stops or overshoot. Outside clicks, including transparent rounded corners, Close and Escape dismiss it. Reduced motion skips flipping. A new award triggers a toast with a 900ms confetti burst, omitted for reduced motion. Reset Badges clears personal progress without erasing saved calls; existing votes become a baseline and do not re-award on reload. Public reader achievements are fictional fixtures in badge-catalog.js and separate from personal activity.

First Call counts an actual In/Out call. Regular Hunter counts five different dates with an actual call; revisiting pages does not count. Curious Mind requires reaching the community panel near the end of articles across five collections. Trend Tracker counts opening unread followed-collection stories on three different dates. Dates use America/New_York. Sharp Eye requires five correct calls with verified outcome records; the prototype has no such records and sample vote splits never qualify. Skipped cards never qualify. Read progress and follow data remain local to this prototype.
