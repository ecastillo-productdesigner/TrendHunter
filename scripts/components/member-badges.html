Member achievement badges

Logo-inspired outlined hexagonal medals use existing DS accent colors and Material Symbols. The full design is 72 × 80px with a 32px icon; profile chips use 24 × 32px medals, a 14px label and a 48px target. Community profiles show up to three earned badges below Member since 2026. View all badges opens criteria and earned dates. The owner can hide earned badges from their profile. A small toast announces a newly earned badge. Reader-profile achievements are clearly identified sample fixtures in badge-catalog.js; personal awards use browser-local activity and never inherit those examples.

First Call counts an actual In/Out call. Regular Hunter counts five different dates with an actual call; revisiting pages does not count. Curious Mind requires reaching the community panel near the end of articles across five collections. Trend Tracker counts opening unread followed-collection stories on three different dates. Dates use America/New_York. Sharp Eye requires five correct calls with verified outcome records; the prototype has no such records and sample vote splits never qualify. Skipped cards never qualify.
