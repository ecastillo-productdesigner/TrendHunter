Member achievement badges

Rounded-square badges use a lime #BEFC4F inset border and Material Symbols over a slate #343841 fill, in both themes. The full badge is 72 × 72px with a 32px icon and --radius-md corners; the current profile badge is 48 × 48px with a 24px icon, overlaid on the lower-right of the 140px avatar. Only the newest visible earned badge appears there. Clicking it opens a square detail popup with --radius-full (100px) corners, a 3px --stroke-thick inset lime border and the shared --color-scrim backdrop. Desktop details use DS Page masthead (40px/36px) for the name, 18px/28px Inter for the description, 14px/22px for dates and 18px actions. On phones the name uses Card headline (28px/28px), Body criteria (16px/22px), Helper metadata (12px/20px) and 16px actions. The symbol is 144px, or 80px on phones. The detail layout spaces the heading, large symbol, description and helper text down the square, with Close and Show all side by side near the bottom. Other members’ badge details offer Close only. On your own badges, Show all flips the same popup to the five-badge collection, distinguishing lime earned icons from gray icons and names for badges to collect. The collection grid sits --spacing-layout-xl (32px) below Member badges on desktop and mobile. It uses aligned two-line name slots, a centered final pair, and consistent state baselines, with 24px between rows on desktop and 16px on phones. Selecting any badge flips to its criteria and progress or earned date. Show recent returns to the newest visible earned badge. The opening reveal matches the incoming view flip: 300ms, half of --motion-card-flip, ease-out, without intermediate stops or overshoot. Outside clicks, including transparent rounded corners, Close and Escape dismiss it. Reduced motion skips flipping. A new award triggers the selected Medal burst: a 72px earned badge above the existing confirmation toast, with 24 lime, purple and mint particles radiating around it for 1200ms. It plays once per new award, then removes the badge preview and particles. Reduced motion retains the toast without animation. Reset Badges clears personal progress without erasing saved calls or bookmarks; existing votes and saves become a baseline and do not re-award on reload. Public reader achievements are fictional fixtures in badge-catalog.js and separate from personal activity.

First Call counts an actual In/Out call. Regular Hunter counts five different dates with an actual call; revisiting pages does not count. Deep Diver requires reaching the end of three different signals. Field Notes counts five distinct saved signals. Repeat reads and saves do not increase progress. Dates use America/New_York. Sharp Eye requires three correct calls with verified outcome records; the prototype has no such records and sample vote splits never qualify. Skipped cards never qualify. Read progress and follow data remain local to this prototype.
