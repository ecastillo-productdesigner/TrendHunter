Feed navigation

The home feed shows For You, Collections, Trending Up and Trending Down beneath the main header. Other pages retain their original single-row navigation. The row uses 16px / 18px light Barlow Condensed, 32px gaps, 24px top padding, a 12px label-to-underline gap and a 3px selected underline. The 18px label line plus padding and underline creates a 57px tab, above the DS minimum 48px target. The row’s 1px bottom border brings its total height to 58px. The active underline follows the DS filter color: lime in dark mode and purple in light mode. The tab group is centered from 960px. On narrow screens the row starts at the left and scrolls horizontally. It stays with the main header. The feed’s sticky offsets include both rows. Other pages retain their original header spacing.

For You shows all 19 stories, prioritizing saved topic interests while keeping the original inline module slots. With no saved interests, stories retain their original order. Collections shows editorial trend cards tied to the followed collections, with no surrounding frame or Following heading. New stories appear first. All combines the followed collections; chips for each followed collection reuse the personal-profile filter style and show only that collection’s stories. The selected collection is stored in the URL for reload and Back. Shared stories appear once in All; all 36 collections have matching prototype articles. With no follows it uses the profile Predictions empty-state typography and links the word collections in “Follow collections to see them here” to Evidence-Based Beauty; Recommended Collections is removed. Both Trending views have one sticky text button with a 24px lower gap. Clicking it switches Biggest rise / Smallest rise in Trending Up and Biggest drop / Smallest drop in Trending Down. Both default to the biggest change first. The native button uses 16px Barlow Condensed and a 48px target, with no dropdown, border or background. Enter and Space also switch the order; the focus outline and live result count make the change accessible. The choice persists in the URL for reload and Back. Trending Up filters rising stories and sorts them by percentage gain, highest first; ties retain their original order. Trending Down filters falling stories and sorts from the largest percentage decline to the smallest; ties retain their original order. Returning to For You restores its saved-interest order. Inline modules are hidden in filtered views; their state is preserved. Selected views survive reload and browser history through the URL hash. Left / Right, Home and End move between tabs. The selected tab and panel are labelled for assistive technology, with a live result count. The daily completion section has a 1px default border and 2px corners, fits its content, with 24px padding on all sides and no fixed aspect ratio or vertical centering. The full footer appears only in For You; Collections, Trending Up and Trending Down hide it.

Following people

The separate Following feed tab shows public activity from followed creators and members with All / Creators / Community filters. The All / Creators / Community filter row floats above its cards while scrolling, matching the collection filters with an opaque surface and 24px bottom padding. Creator articles use the same editorial card; member predictions use the shared comparison row. Signed-in visitors who have not voted see Make Your Call below the member’s badge. It opens the same In / Out choices used on creator profiles; submitting saves the personal vote and shows You’re in / out without changing the member’s call. Signed-out visitors have no call control. Context labels use the same 16px/18px regular Barlow Condensed typography as recommendations and are left-aligned with the card content. They name the person, such as Daniel published a new story or Jamie made a prediction. Activity is illustrative, without fake live timestamps. Each creator and member profile has Follow / Following in the Edit Profile position. Signed-out Follow actions open the login screen, remember the person, then return to that profile and complete the follow with a confirmation toast. The owner keeps Edit Profile and gets a Following tab for managing people, with the same filters and an Undo toast on unfollow. Saved articles are not broadcast as activity. Collections filters remain separate and sticky over the story cards, with exactly 24px from filter pills to the first card and an opaque 24px lower gap while sticky.
