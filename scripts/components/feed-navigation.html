Feed navigation

Every content page shows For You, Collections, Trending Up and Trending Down beneath the main header. The home feed uses working tabs; article, collection, channel, contributor, profile, prediction and Answers pages use links to the matching feed view. Collections and channels highlight Collections; other content pages highlight For You. Authentication pages retain their focused header. The row uses 16px / 18px light Barlow Condensed, 32px gaps, 24px top padding, a 12px label-to-underline gap and a 3px selected underline. The 18px label line plus padding and underline creates a 57px tab, above the DS minimum 48px target. The row’s 1px bottom border brings its total height to 58px. The active underline follows the DS filter color: lime in dark mode and purple in light mode. The tab group is centered from 960px. On narrow screens the row starts at the left and scrolls horizontally. It stays with the main header. Shared sticky offsets include both rows, so rails, article panel edges and scroll targets clear the full header.

For You restores the original 19 stories and inline modules. Collections shows all 36 linked collections with their assigned interest values. Trending Up filters rising stories and sorts them by percentage gain, highest first; ties retain their original order. Trending Down filters falling stories. Returning to For You restores the original story and module order. Inline modules are hidden in these filtered views; their state is preserved. Selected views survive reload and browser history through the URL hash. Left / Right, Home and End move between tabs. The selected tab and panel are labelled for assistive technology, with a live result count.
