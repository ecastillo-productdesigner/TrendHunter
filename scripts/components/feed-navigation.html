Feed navigation

The home feed shows For You, Collections, Trending Up and Trending Down beneath the main header. Other pages retain their original single-row navigation. The row uses 16px / 18px light Barlow Condensed, 32px gaps, 24px top padding, a 12px label-to-underline gap and a 3px selected underline. The 18px label line plus padding and underline creates a 57px tab, above the DS minimum 48px target. The row’s 1px bottom border brings its total height to 58px. The active underline follows the DS filter color: lime in dark mode and purple in light mode. The tab group is centered from 960px. On narrow screens the row starts at the left and scrolls horizontally. It stays with the main header. The feed’s sticky offsets include both rows. Other pages retain their original header spacing.

For You shows all 19 stories, prioritizing saved topic interests while keeping the original inline module slots. With no saved interests, stories retain their original order. Collections shows editorial trend cards tied to the followed collections, with no surrounding frame or Following heading. New stories appear first. All combines the followed collections; chips for each followed collection reuse the personal-profile filter style and show only that collection’s stories. The selected collection is stored in the URL for reload and Back. Shared stories appear once in All; all 36 collections have matching prototype articles. With no follows it uses the profile Predictions empty-state typography and links the word collections in “Follow collections to see them here” to Evidence-Based Beauty; Recommended Collections is removed. Trending Up filters rising stories and sorts them by percentage gain, highest first; ties retain their original order. Trending Down filters falling stories and sorts from the smallest percentage decline to the largest; ties retain their original order. Returning to For You restores its saved-interest order. Inline modules are hidden in filtered views; their state is preserved. Selected views survive reload and browser history through the URL hash. Left / Right, Home and End move between tabs. The selected tab and panel are labelled for assistive technology, with a live result count. The full footer appears only in For You; Collections, Trending Up and Trending Down hide it.

New content in followed collections

Occasional sample releases in four collections drive the same unread state across the red Collections-tab dot, a red count tag on personal-profile collection pills and a small New in a collection you follow label on matching For You stories. The tag names the content, such as 3 new stories. Opening Collections acknowledges the current updates and immediately clears the navigation dot, including when using the keyboard or a direct Collections link. Per-collection story labels remain until the matching article is viewed. A separate browser-local acknowledgment keeps the dot cleared on reload and Back; later unseen releases can show it again. Unfollowing immediately removes that collection from the signal. Red uses --color-content-new (#EF4444), with a dark 14px bold badge label for contrast and an 8px dot. Counts are included in the tab’s accessible name. The example releases are fixed browser-local prototype data, not timed or random notifications.

Following people

The separate Following feed tab shows public activity from followed creators and members with All / Creators / Members filters. Creator articles use the same editorial card; member predictions use the existing read-only call row. Context labels name the person, such as Daniel published a new story or Jamie made a prediction. Activity is illustrative, without fake live timestamps. Each creator and member profile has Follow / Following in the Edit Profile position. Signed-out Follow actions open the login screen, remember the person, then return to that profile and complete the follow with a confirmation toast. The owner keeps Edit Profile and gets a Following tab for managing people, with the same filters and an Undo toast on unfollow. Saved articles are not broadcast as activity. Collections filters remain separate and sticky over the story cards, with exactly 24px from filter pills to the first card.
