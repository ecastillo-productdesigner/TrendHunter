Experiences

Experiences explores the ways people are choosing to spend their time together and the places, rituals and communities giving those choices a new shape. It follows the run club that becomes a social calendar, the trip built around participation rather than a checklist, and the gathering that makes room for different ways to join in. From neighborhood events to independent travel, this channel looks at the details that turn an appealing idea into a memorable experience: the welcome, the setting, the plan and the people. Come here to discover emerging formats, understand what draws people to them and find a different way to spend your next free afternoon or your next adventure.
